- colour values are "#RRGGBB" only. Never a CSS string, a gradient expression or

  "ring", "border" and "chevron" need "borderColor" and a "borderWidth" of 1 to

- the field the text sits on must be owned by something. A flat colour area
  behind the copy is a "panel" shape; a darkening that fades across the
  composition is a "gradient" shape; a part of the photograph that simply is
