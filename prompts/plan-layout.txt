WHAT TO PRODUCE
1. textLayers: one per copy item, with real typography. Hebrew uses direction
   "rtl"; a column's x and width stay where the composition needs them whatever
   the direction. Latin contact details use "ltr". Choose fontFamily only from:
   {fonts}
   An outlined headline is real text with strokeColor and strokeWidth (fill
   "solid"), or fill "transparent" for hollow lettering. letterSpacing in px.
   wrap "nowrap" only for a line that must not break; overflow "shrink".
2. shapeLayers: draw simple elements IN CODE, never in the photograph. Dark
   panels and readability gradients, call-to-action backgrounds, circles, dots,
   rings, borders, chevrons and basic badges. Each shape names an `effect` id that
   it implements; declare every effect in `effects` with owner "layout". There may
   be only ONE readabilityGradient (kind "readabilityGradient"). A gradient has
   `stops` (colour, opacity 0..1, position 0..1) and an `angle` in CSS degrees
   (180 runs top to bottom). Use at most 4 stops. A ring, border or chevron MUST have a borderColor (#RRGGBB) and a borderWidth above 0; a panel, circle or dot MUST have a color. Use [] stops for non-gradients.
3. imageLayers: exactly ONE, of type "background" with assetId null, the
   full-canvas photograph at zIndex 0. No other image layers.
4. Stack: photograph (0) < shapes < text. Text must remain readable over what is
   under it: that is what the gradients and panels are for.
5. photoBrief: a description of ONLY the photograph - subject, setting, lighting,
   colour, composition and where the calm areas are for text and panels. It must
   describe a photograph with NO text, letters, numbers, logos, borders, panels,
   gradients, badges or any graphic overlay: those are drawn by the layout.
